How IraChat Works

Three simple ideas power a private, offline-first AI experience — no servers, no logging, no interruptions.

1
memory

Local Model Execution

IraChat leverages lightweight, optimized language models that run locally using your device's own hardware, bypassing cloud servers entirely.

2
cloud_off

Zero Data Transmission

Because processing happens completely on-device, your personal study habits, notes, and prompts never get sent over the internet or logged anywhere.

3
bolt

Seamless Offline Support

Lost connection in class or on the move? IraChat keeps working smoothly without skipping a beat, so your productivity is never interrupted.

Ready when you are.

Your questions. Your learning. Your AI. Online or offline, IraChat is ready.
