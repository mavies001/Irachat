How IraChat Works

1. Local Model Execution

IraChat leverages lightweight optimized language models that run locally using your device's hardware computation capabilities, bypassing cloud servers entirely.

2. Zero Data Transmission

Because processing happens completely on-device, your personal study habits, notes, and prompts never get sent over the internet or logged anywhere.

3. Seamless Offline Support

Lost connection in class or on the move? IraChat keeps working smoothly without skipping a beat, ensuring your productivity is uninterrupted.
